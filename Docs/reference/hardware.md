# Hardware

## Main Components

- MCU: [STM32H7S3Z8](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32h7s3z8.html)
- DSP: [ADAU1466](https://www.analog.com/en/products/adau1466.html)
- Audio codec: [AK4619](https://www.akm.com/global/en/products/audio/audio-codec/ak4619vn/)
- Headphone DAC: [TAD5142](https://www.ti.com/product/TAD5142)
- Controls: 10 × [Gateron Low Profile Magnetic Jade Pro](https://www.gateron.com/products/gateron-full-pom-low-profile-magnetic-jade-pro-switch-set) switches and 12 × 10 kΩ potentiometers
